\section{Equations}
\label{sec:equations}

Equations can be written inline, such as $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, or displayed as follows:

\begin{equation}
    E = mc^2
    \label{eq:einstein}
\end{equation}

You can refer to equations using \verb|\ref{}| or \verb|\cref{}|, for example \cref{eq:einstein}.
